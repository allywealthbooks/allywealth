06 - BOOK MARKETING GLOSSARY

Understanding the language
of publishing.

A quick reference guide to some of the most common terms you will encounter in the book marketing industry.

ARC (Advance Reader Copy)

A free copy of a book given to readers or reviewers before the official publication date, intended to generate early feedback and reviews.

ASIN (Amazon Standard Identification Number)

A unique 10-character alphanumeric identifier assigned by Amazon to products on its platform, used for tracking and listing.

Backend Keywords

Hidden search terms entered in the publisher dashboard (like KDP) that help the platform's algorithm understand what your book is about.

Blurb

The promotional description of a book, usually found on the back cover or the retail product page, designed to entice readers.

BookTok

A community on TikTok dedicated to discussing and recommending books, which has become a massive driver of book sales.

Conversion Rate

The percentage of visitors to your book's page who actually complete a purchase. Optimizing this is crucial for profitability.

KDP (Kindle Direct Publishing)

Amazon's self-publishing platform for independent authors to publish ebooks and print-on-demand paperbacks.

A+ Content

An Amazon feature allowing authors to add enhanced images and text to their product pages to increase conversion.
