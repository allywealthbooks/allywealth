THE BRIEF

Stalled at launch, invisible after week two.

The author had done everything a debut novelist is told to do. The book was professionally edited, the cover had been commissioned, and the launch week had been announced to a modest mailing list. It sold reasonably for ten days and then went quiet.

Our brief was to work out why a book with strong editorial reviews was not converting page visits into sales, and to rebuild the listing so that future promotional efforts would actually produce results.

The first audit showed three separate problems. The cover read well at full size but was illegible as a thumbnail. The description opened with a plot summary instead of a hook. And the book was placed in two categories that were technically accurate but far too competitive for a debut to rank in.

2 new Attainable category placements
41% Lift in organic impressions

Amazon

Backlist revival for a five book business series

A non-fiction author with five books in a single business topic had seen sales decline steadily for eighteen months. A full category and keyword audit revealed that three of the five were in categories that had become far too competitive. We repositioned two of the strongest titles into smaller but highly relevant categories and rewrote their descriptions to reflect how the market had shifted.

Launch

Ninety day launch campaign for a memoir

A memoir with a strong personal story but no existing audience needed a launch strategy that did not rely on a large mailing list. We built a phased campaign around a small group of committed early readers, a series of short public reading events, and a coordinated outreach to podcasters in the memoir and personal development space. Launch week was quiet. The four weeks after it were not.

Branding

Visual identity rebuild for a science fiction author

A science fiction author with a strong voice and an inconsistent visual presence needed a brand that could travel across seven books. We built a restrained visual system: two typefaces, a fixed colour palette, and a consistent composition rule for every graphic. Every asset the author published for the following year looked like it belonged to the same body of work.

Discovery

Reader community mapping for a romance debut

A debut romance author had a book that reviewers loved and a marketing budget that could not compete with the big publishers. We mapped the reader communities where the book had the strongest natural fit, prioritised three of them, and built a slow, respectful engagement strategy that avoided paid promotion entirely.

ARC

Compliant ARC campaign for a literary debut

A literary fiction author wanted early reviews without risking their account or their reputation. We built an ARC programme around a curated list of readers who had reviewed similar titles in the previous twelve months. Every invitation was personal, every reviewer opted in voluntarily, and the resulting reviews were honest, varied, and durable.

42 ARC readers recruited

Audiobook

Audiobook promotion for a self-help author

An author with a successful print and ebook presence had an audiobook that was essentially invisible. We built a promotion strategy that treated the audio edition as a standalone product, with its own listener audience, its own outreach list of audio focused podcasts, and its own set of promotional assets. The audio edition went on to outsell the ebook edition in its second quarter.
