02 - SERVICES AND PROCESS

How we work.

Timelines vary based on the services required. A listing optimisation might take a week, while a full launch campaign typically spans 8 to 12 weeks. We provide a detailed timeline during the proposal stage, ensuring you know exactly what to expect at every stage of the process.

No. No ethical marketer can guarantee sales or rankings, as these are determined by the platform's algorithms and reader behavior. We guarantee strategic, evidence-aware work that positions your book optimally for its target audience. We focus on improving discoverability and conversion, which are the foundational drivers of long-term sales.

We will need your manuscript details, any existing marketing assets, your author bio, and access to your book listing where necessary. We will provide a complete onboarding checklist once we begin.

We establish clear, measurable goals at the outset. This might include improved listing conversion rates, increased keyword visibility, successful ARC campaign execution, or higher engagement on social channels. We provide transparent reporting on these metrics and review them with you regularly.

For larger, multi-stage campaigns, we often structure payments into milestones. We will discuss the specific payment schedule during the proposal process to ensure it aligns with your budget.

03 - ETHICS AND COMPLIANCE

Our commitment to integrity.

Yes. We strictly adhere to ethical guidelines. We never buy reviews, manipulate ratings, or pressure readers. Our outreach focuses on connecting your book with genuinely interested readers who voluntarily choose to leave honest feedback. We encourage all recipients to disclose that they received a free copy.

Never. We believe in authentic, human-to-human engagement. Using bots or automated engagement violates the terms of service of most platforms and damages your author brand. We engage with readers respectfully and manually.

Readers are entitled to their opinions. We never argue with reviewers, identify them, or mobilize followers against criticism. We use negative feedback as valuable insight to understand reader expectations and potential manuscript issues, respecting the reader's perspective.
