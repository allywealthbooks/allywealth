WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

From here, in three steps.

There is nothing for you to chase. Here is exactly what happens between now and the moment you hear back from us.

We read your enquiry

Your message lands in a shared inbox that both founders read directly. No ticket system, no autoresponder, no outsourcing. A real person opens it, reads it in full, and makes a note of what you are looking for.

01

We review your book

We look at the book, its category, its listing, and its current market position. If we can already see the highest impact fix, we will mention it in our reply. If we need more context, we will ask for it directly.

02

We reply with an honest assessment

Within 24 to 48 business hours you will hear back from us. The reply will either be a proposal, a short set of clarifying questions, or, occasionally, an honest note that we are not the right fit for this particular project.

SETTING EXPECTATIONS

What you can count on from us.

These are the commitments we make to every author who reaches out, whether or not the enquiry turns into a project.

Response within 24 to 48 hours

Enquiries sent during the week are usually answered the same or next business day. Weekend enquiries are answered on Monday.

A reply from a real person

You will hear from one of the founders, not a template. Every reply is written specifically for your enquiry.

Honesty, even when it is not the answer you wanted

If we do not think we can help, we will tell you and, where we can, suggest an alternative. We never take on a project we cannot do well.
