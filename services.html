Amazon Book Marketing

The Problem: You have published your book on Amazon, but it is lost in a sea of millions of titles. You are not getting the organic impressions or clicks you expected.

Our Approach: We audit your entire listing, from backend metadata to front-facing copy, and analyze your top competitors. We build a comprehensive discoverability strategy to position your book in front of high-intent buyers.

  • Listing Audits: A deep review of your current title, subtitle, description, and categories.
  • Competitor Analysis: We study the books ranking in your target categories to identify keyword gaps and positioning opportunities.
  • Discoverability Strategy: A step-by-step plan to improve your ranking and visibility on the platform.
