OUR STORY

Built for the authors the industry overlooks.

Ally Wealth began with a simple observation. The publishing world is full of talented authors who have done everything right. They finished the manuscript. They commissioned a cover. They published on Amazon. And then they waited, because nobody told them what comes next.

Marketing advice for authors is plentiful, but most of it is either too generic to apply or too expensive to access. The big agencies focus on authors with existing audiences. The free advice focuses on tactics without context. Somewhere in the middle, a huge number of serious authors are left to piece together a strategy from blog posts and conflicting opinions.

We built Ally Wealth to sit in that middle. Premium thinking, delivered with the clarity and directness of a specialist rather than the bureaucracy of an agency. We work with a limited number of authors at a time, because visibility work requires attention, and attention does not scale.

Our approach is deliberately unglamorous. We audit what exists. We fix the foundations. We measure what changes. We keep what works. There are no secret formulas and no promises of overnight success, because the authors who sell well over time are the ones who built a system and stuck with it.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

The principles behind our work.

These are not slogans. They are the standards we hold ourselves to on every project, and the reasons our clients trust us with their books.

Honesty over hype

We will never promise a bestseller ranking. We will tell you what is realistic for your book, your genre, and your stage. If a service will not help you, we will say so, even if it costs us the project.

Ethics before shortcuts

Every ARC campaign, every outreach message, and every review strategy follows the rules of the platforms we work with. We would rather grow slowly and safely than risk your account on a shortcut.

Clarity in everything

You will always know what we are doing, why we are doing it, and what it costs. No hidden line items, no jargon designed to confuse, and no reports that look impressive but say nothing.

A limited client list

We only take on a small number of authors at a time. This is deliberate. Visibility work is detail work, and detail does not survive a workload that is too large. When you work with us, you have our attention.

Measured, not guessed

Every campaign has numbers attached to it. We track what matters, we report on it plainly, and we change course when the data tells us to. Marketing is a series of decisions, not a leap of faith.

Built for the long term

A launch is a moment. A career is a decade. We build strategies that keep working after the launch window closes, because the goal is not one good week. It is a body of work that readers keep finding.

HOW WE WORK

A process built on evidence.

Every engagement follows the same four stages. The scale changes depending on the service, but the logic does not. Find the truth, fix the foundation, build the campaign, then measure what happened.

01

Audit

We start by looking at what already exists. Your listing, your categories, your description, your author profile, and the competitive landscape around your book. Most marketing problems are visible at this stage, and most are fixable before money is spent on promotion.

02

Foundation

Before we try to bring anyone to your book, the book page has to convert the people who arrive. We rewrite, restructure, and reposition the fundamentals so that traffic, when it comes, actually turns into sales rather than a quick bounce.

03

Campaign

With the foundation in place, we build the campaign. That might be a launch timeline, an ARC programme, an audiobook push, a social strategy, or a combination. Each element is chosen because it fits your book, not because it is fashionable.

04

Measure

We track the numbers that matter, report on them in plain language, and adjust. The strongest strategies are not the ones that predicted everything correctly. They are the ones that responded quickly when the data disagreed with the plan.

WHO WE WORK WITH

The right fit matters.

We are not the right consultancy for every author. These are the people we do our best work with, and the reasons why.

Debut and early career authors

You have finished or nearly finished your first book and want to start with a foundation that will not need rebuilding later. We will be honest about what is realistic at your stage and help you avoid the common first-launch mistakes.

Established authors with a backlist

Your earlier books are still selling but have plateaued. A backlist can be revived with the right positioning, and often the work is smaller than authors expect. We focus on the highest impact changes first.

Non-fiction authors and experts

You have a message, a method, or a body of expertise, and the book is one part of a wider professional identity. We build visibility that supports your broader work, not just a single title.

Independent and hybrid publishers

You are publishing under your own imprint or through a hybrid model and need marketing strategy that fits the independence you fought for. We work with the constraints of independent publishing, not against them.

What we are not: a guarantee factory, a click farm, or a service that buys reviews. If you are looking for a fast route to a bestseller badge, we are not the right partner. If you are looking to build something that lasts, we probably are.
